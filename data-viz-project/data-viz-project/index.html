Student usage of recreational facilities like the gym or pool has rebounded five years post pandemic

By Zephan Matteson

April 20, 2026

In 2020 the usage of recreational facilites at UMD such as the gym or pool plummeted. In the five years since, student usage of these facilites has rebounded to pre-pandemic levels. Additionally there have been some major demographic changes in those five years with the percentage of Asian students making use of these facilites majorly increasing.
